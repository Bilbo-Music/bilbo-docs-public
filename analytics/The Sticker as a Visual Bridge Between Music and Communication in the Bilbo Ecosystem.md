# Research: The Sticker as a Visual Bridge Between Music and Communication in the Bilbo Ecosystem

## Introduction
In traditional streaming services, the "Like" button is a cold transaction in a database. At Bilbo, we view every user interaction with content as an **emotional act**. Integrating animated TGS (Telegram Game Stickers) into interface mechanics transforms a static application into a living, responsive environment.

---

## 1. The Hypothesis: The Sticker as an "Emotional Anchor"
We operate on the premise that music is more than just sound—it is a visual code. 
**The Mechanics:** When an artist customizes reactions (likes, reposts, donations) with their unique TGS packs, they create a persistent neural connection:
*   **Action:** Liking a track.
*   **Reaction:** A unique animation triggers (e.g., the artist's character or an album symbol).
*   **Result:** This emotion carries over into private chats as the user begins using that same sticker pack within Telegram/MAX.

---

## 2. Interaction Scenarios (The Tip of the Iceberg)

### A. Dynamic Profile Personalization
Artists assign a "mood" to their page. For an aggressive release, they might set a pack with high-energy graphics; for a lo-fi beat, a relaxed, chill animation. This turns every artist profile in Bilbo into a unique visual world.

### B. Social Proof & Loyalty
Stickers act as digital rewards:
*   **Achievement Packs:** A "Top Listener" sticker that triggers and unlocks in the messenger only after the user listens to an artist's track for the 100th time.
*   **Donation Effects:** Contributing through the `ROYALITY` system triggers an exclusive animation that cannot be obtained any other way.

### C. Event-Driven Marketing (Release Cycles)
The launch of an album is accompanied by a sticker pack release. The sticker becomes a "free sample" of the artist's brand, which fans naturally distribute across the network, integrating the music into their daily conversations.

---

## 3. Deep Dive: New Opportunities (The Body of the Iceberg)

### Layer 1: Timeline Stickers
Artists can place triggers directly on the track's timeline. At 01:20 (the moment of climax), the listener's screen fills with flying stickers, creating an augmented reality effect right inside the player.

### Layer 2: Interactive Merch (Digital Loot)
Within the `MERCH` concept, a sticker pack is a digital good with zero distribution cost. This allows for:
*   Selling access to "Premium Reaction Animations."
*   Gifting exclusive stickers to buyers of physical merchandise.

### Layer 3: Cross-Platform Virality
A user takes a sticker from Bilbo into Telegram/MAX. Another user, seeing this sticker in a chat, can use the sticker's context menu (or the link in the pack description) to jump directly to the artist's profile in Bilbo. **The sticker becomes an entry point into the marketing funnel.**

---

## 4. Technical Aspect: Why TGS?
The choice of the TGS (Telegram Game Stickers) format is driven by:
1.  **Lightweight Performance:** Minimal file size with high smoothness (60 FPS).
2.  **Vector-Based:** Perfect rendering on any screen resolution.
3.  **Native Integration:** Full compatibility with Bilbo's primary environment—messengers.

---

## Summary: The "Live Like" Formula
**Music + Custom Animation = Loyalty.**

We are transforming a UI element into a tool for artist self-expression. In the world of Bilbo, a "Like" is not just a statistic—it is a greeting from the artist that the fan carries away in their pocket.
